CITY COMMUNITY LIBRARY — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
=====================================================

MEMBERSHIP
----------

Q: How do I become a library member?
A: You can register for membership at the library front desk during opening hours, or online via our website. You will need a valid government-issued photo ID and proof of address. Membership is processed within 1 working day. You will receive your membership card by email (digital) or in person (physical card).

Q: What membership plans are available?
A: We offer four membership plans:
   - Student: Rs 200/year. Available for enrolled students with valid ID. Borrow up to 3 books at a time.
   - Standard: Rs 500/year. For working adults. Borrow up to 5 books at a time.
   - Senior: Rs 250/year. For members aged 60 and above with valid age proof. Borrow up to 5 books at a time.
   - Premium: Rs 1000/year. Unlimited borrowing, priority reservations, early access to new arrivals, free event registration.

Q: Can I upgrade my membership mid-year?
A: Yes. You can upgrade your membership at any time. You will pay the difference in fees pro-rated for the remaining months. Visit the front desk or contact us by email to request an upgrade.

Q: What happens when my membership expires?
A: You will receive a reminder email 30 days before expiry and again 7 days before expiry. After expiry you cannot borrow new books but can return existing ones. Renew at the front desk or online. Books borrowed before expiry may still be returned without penalty if within their due date.

Q: Can I have a membership for my family?
A: Each family member needs their own membership account. However, we offer a Family Bundle at Rs 1500/year for up to 4 members in the same household, each on the Standard plan.

Q: How do I cancel my membership?
A: Contact the front desk or email us at library@citylib.in. Please return all borrowed books before cancellation. No refund is provided for unused months unless cancellation is due to relocation outside the city.

BORROWING BOOKS
---------------

Q: How many books can I borrow at one time?
A: This depends on your membership:
   - Student: 3 books
   - Standard: 5 books
   - Senior: 5 books
   - Premium: Unlimited (subject to availability)

Q: How long can I keep a borrowed book?
A: The standard loan period is 14 days from the date of borrowing. Premium members may request a 21-day loan period at the time of borrowing.

Q: Can I renew a book?
A: Yes, you can renew a book once online or at the front desk, as long as no other member has reserved the same book and the book is not overdue. Renewals extend the due date by 7 days from the original due date.

Q: Can I reserve a book that is currently on loan?
A: Yes. You can place a reservation for any book that is currently on loan. You will be notified by email or SMS when the book becomes available. Reservations are held for 3 days after notification.

Q: Can I borrow e-books?
A: Yes. Premium members have access to our e-book collection through the library app. E-books are borrowed for 14 days and automatically returned at the end of the loan period. No physical return required.

Q: What happens if I lose a book?
A: Please report the loss to the front desk immediately. You will be charged the replacement cost of the book plus a processing fee of Rs 50. The replacement cost is based on the current market price of the book.

RETURNING BOOKS
---------------

Q: Where do I return books?
A: Books can be returned at the front desk during opening hours (Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM; Sunday, 10 AM to 5 PM). We also have a 24-hour book drop slot outside the main entrance for returns after hours. Books placed in the drop slot are processed the next working day.

Q: What if I return a book after the due date?
A: A late fee of Rs 5 per day applies for each day a book is overdue, up to a maximum of Rs 200. After 30 days overdue, the book is considered lost and you will be charged the replacement cost. See our Late Return Policy for full details.

Q: Can I return a book borrowed by a family member?
A: Yes, but the late fees and any penalties remain the responsibility of the account holder who borrowed the book.

Q: What happens if I return a damaged book?
A: Minor damage such as minor cover wear is acceptable. Significant damage including torn pages, water damage, or writing inside the book will result in a damage fee. The fee is assessed by a librarian and ranges from Rs 50 to the full replacement cost depending on the extent of damage.

NEW ARRIVALS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------------

Q: How do I find out about new arrivals?
A: Subscribe to our monthly newsletter by providing your email at the front desk or on our website. New arrivals are also listed on our website and notice board at the library entrance.

Q: How do I request a book that is not in the collection?
A: Fill in a Book Request Form at the front desk or submit it online. We review all requests and purchase popular titles when budget allows. You will be notified if the book is added to the collection.

Q: Can you recommend a book for me?
A: Yes! Our Library Helper chatbot (available on the library website and WhatsApp) can recommend books based on your reading history and interests. You can also ask a librarian for personalised recommendations at the front desk.

Q: How often are new books added to the collection?
A: We add new books every month. The library budget allows for approximately 50-100 new titles per quarter. Priority is given to member requests, recent award winners, and popular authors.

EVENTS AND WORKSHOPS
--------------------

Q: How do I register for a library event?
A: You can register for events through our website, at the front desk, or by calling our helpline at 1800-LIB-HELP. Some events are first-come-first-served and do not require registration.

Q: Are events free for members?
A: Most events are free for active members. Some specialised workshops (such as Power Platform training) may have a small fee. Fees are listed clearly in the event description.

Q: Can non-members attend events?
A: Yes, non-members can attend most events. However, members get priority registration and may receive discounts on paid events.

Q: Where can I find the event calendar?
A: The event calendar is available on our website, at the library front desk, and in our monthly newsletter.

LIBRARY RULES AND CONDUCT
--------------------------

Q: What are the library's opening hours?
A: Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Sunday: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed on national public holidays. Special hours apply during festivals.

Q: Is there a quiet zone in the library?
A: Yes. The Reading Lounge on the first floor is a designated quiet zone. Mobile phones must be on silent and conversations should be kept to a whisper. The ground floor is more relaxed and suitable for group study.

Q: Can I bring food and drinks into the library?
A: Water in sealed bottles is permitted. Food and other drinks are not allowed inside the library premises to protect the books and maintain cleanliness.

Q: Is there Wi-Fi available?
A: Yes. Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the library. Connect to the network "CityLib-Guest" using the password available at the front desk. The connection is filtered and monitored in accordance with our internet use policy.

Q: Are children allowed in the library?
A: Yes. Children are welcome and have a dedicated Children's Corner with age-appropriate books, activities, and seating. Children under 10 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.

LIBRARY HELPER CHATBOT
-----------------------

Q: What can the Library Helper chatbot do?
A: The Library Helper can:
   - Check if a book is available
   - Tell you the due date of your borrowed books
   - Help you renew a book
   - Recommend books based on your interests
   - Tell you about upcoming events
   - Answer questions about library policies
   - Look up an author's other works

Q: Is the Library Helper available 24 hours?
A: Yes. The chatbot is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week through our website and WhatsApp. For queries requiring a human librarian, please visit the front desk or call during opening hours.

Q: How do I access the Library Helper on WhatsApp?
A: Save the number +91-9000-LIB-BOT to your contacts and send a message saying "Hello" to get started.
