When something goes wrong

ProblemTry this
"It signed me in as the wrong account."Close everything, open a fresh incognito window, sign in again with the learning account.
"I can't see my table / app."Check the environment picker (top-right) says Learning. Things live per-environment.
"My flow didn't run."Open the flow's Run history. A red step tells you exactly what failed; click it to read the message.
"A formula has a red underline."Read the tooltip — usually a missing bracket/quote, or a name typed wrong. Quotes must be straight ".
"The chatbot won't answer from my document."Knowledge sources take a minute or two to index after upload. Wait, then retry in the Test panel.
"Azure is asking about payment / I'm worried about cost."Stop. Don't create virtual machines. Delete rg-learning. Do Azure with the account owner.
"A blue 'delegation' warning appeared."Harmless for your small practice data. It only matters with thousands of rows — a topic for later.
"Power BI won't let me model / write DAX easily."The browser Service is limited; install free Power BI Desktop (Windows) when you have a laptop for full modelling and DAX.
"My progress ticks disappeared."Ticks are saved per browser/computer and per page. A different device or clearing browser data resets them — that's expected.
Tip — the single best habit When stuck, slow down and read the message on screen. Power Platform is unusually good at telling you what's wrong in plain-ish English. Nine times out of ten the fix is in the words you skipped.
